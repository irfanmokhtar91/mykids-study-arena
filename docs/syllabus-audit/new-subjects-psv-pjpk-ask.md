Pendidikan Jasmani bidang (DSKP): Gimnastik Asas; Pergerakan Berirama; Kategori Serangan: Bola Baling; Kategori Serangan: Ragbi Sentuh; Kategori Jaring: Sepak Takraw; Kategori Pukul dan Memadang: Kriket; Olahraga Asas; Rekreasi dan Kesenggangan; Kecergasan Fizikal Berdasarkan Kesihatan (rumus Karvonen KNL = [(KNM - KNR) x % intensiti + KNR], prinsip FITT, PNF, SEGAK).

## Honest limits (to show in the app too)
- Written questions only test knowledge; they cannot test drawing, sport skill or practical performance.
- PSV and PJPK have no national paper: practice only, no graded Mock Exam. ASK Tingkatan 2 can later support a Mock Exam once Bahagian A/B pools pass verification.
- Pendidikan Kesihatan Reproduktif topics (puberty, etc.) will follow the textbook wording only, kept age-appropriate for each year.

## Proposed question volume (per chapter, original, objective)
About 20 multiple-choice items per chapter, difficulty tagged 5:3:2, plus ASK Bahagian B-style items (benar/salah, padanan) where the DSKP supports them. Every item goes through the blind-solve gate (shuffled options, independent solvers) and a DSKP scope check before it ships.
